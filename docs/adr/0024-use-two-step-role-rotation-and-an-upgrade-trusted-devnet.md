Governance proposes replacements for governance, operator, Pause Authority, and Override Authority, the incoming key must accept, operational roles cannot rotate themselves, and every proposal or acceptance is evented. A dedicated cold upgrade key may remain through the earlier proof-of-concept milestones with its program-data address, binary hash, and deployment slot published. The mandatory final-demo gate uses the immutable Squads Protocol V4 deployment `SQDS4ep65T869zMMBKyuUq6aD6EgTu8psMjkvj52pCf`, audited source commit `64af7330413d5c85cbbccfd8c27a05d45b6e666f`, and exact `@sqds/multisig@2.1.4`. An autonomous three-member, threshold-two, `configAuthority = null`, zero-devnet-timelock multisig assigns its vault-index-0 PDA—not the multisig account—as Upgradeable Loader authority. Acceptance proves one approval cannot execute and two can perform a hash-verified loader upgrade. Every non-demo deployment requires multisignature upgrade control and a separate approved multisig vault as Override Authority.
