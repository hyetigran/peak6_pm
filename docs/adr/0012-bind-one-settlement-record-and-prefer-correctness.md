# Bind one Settlement Record and prefer correctness over latency

Every Outcome Market for one ticker and Trading Day consumes the same atomically bound Settlement Record rather than separately fetched price and observation-time feeds, and accepts it only when the Settlement Quality Predicate passes. The service preflights five minutes before the regular-session close, begins final-record polling fifteen minutes after close, may settle automatically no earlier than twenty minutes after close, and raises a settlement-SLO incident at twenty-five minutes; after one hour the delayed evidenced override becomes additionally eligible. The immutable record header enforces the twenty-minute earliest-Settlement gate on-chain; the other thresholds are operational, and none is an acceptance expiry, so a trustworthy normal result remains acceptable until Settlement. The twenty-minute cutoff is devnet-specific; any non-demo deployment must first freeze a proven finalized Official Close source or adopt a longer dispute window. If evidence never converges, the market remains Settlement Disputed indefinitely: Pair Redemption stays available while unmatched positions remain locked, and no void or discretionary fallback is invented. Settlement is immutable against later corrections, which produce incident annotations rather than payout changes. Atomic binding, provider finality, shared consumption, and stale-value resistance are non-waiverable M0 gates.
