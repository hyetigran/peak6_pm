# Trigger keeper actions on a schedule, not a polling loop

The automation service's work is almost entirely **time-known** or **event-known**, so it must be driven by a durable scheduler and account subscriptions — not the per-second polling loop the localnet demo uses. The daily lifecycle (PRD §5) already fixes the clock: the day's Outcome Markets are generated and created in a morning window, and settlement runs in a `close+15m…+25m` window once the Official Close is accepted. These are two scheduled jobs a day per trading day, not a condition to be rediscovered 86,400 times. Polling "is it time yet?" every second is wasted RPC and, as implemented with `setInterval(loop, TICK)`, re-enters when a tick outruns its interval and re-sends settle/consume transactions.

**Decision.** Each keeper responsibility is triggered by the substrate that matches its nature:

- **Settlement runner** — fires from the scheduler at `close_ts + normal_settlement_delay_secs`, gated on the Official Close actually being published (Nasdaq posts minutes after 16:00 ET). If the feed is not yet available it reschedules with backoff rather than spinning; it drives `finalize_settlement_normal` (reading the Switchboard delivery feed, ADR-0021/0023/0030) then `settle_market` per Outcome Market.
- **Market-creation / `add_strike` runner** — fires from the scheduler in the morning generate→create→attach window (PRD §5, §6 strike engine).
- **EventHeap cranking** — event-driven, not polled. Because V1 settles maker fills **inline-first** (ID-007, ARCH §8.3), the heap is empty in the common path; an `onAccountChange` subscription cranks only when a heap actually grows, any residual drain is folded into the settlement preflight (the heap must be empty before `settle_market`), and a low-frequency reconcile poll (minutes, not seconds) backstops a missed subscription event. The §8.4 heap-depth SLOs are monitoring thresholds over this subscription, not a busy-poll.

**Why at-least-once scheduling is safe.** Every action is idempotent on-chain, so the scheduler needs only at-least-once delivery and may freely retry: `finalize_settlement_normal` finalizes one shared Settlement Record and no-ops once `FinalOracle` (ADR-0023); `settle_market` re-checks record/market state; market and venue creation are unique-`(ticker,day[,strike])` PDAs that fail closed on repeat; `consume_events` is bounded and idempotent. This also fails closed on indexer unavailability (ADR-0019) — the runner verifies state on-chain before acting.

**Consequences.** The production automation topology is a durable scheduler (giving retries/backoff, observability, and no overlapping-tick hazard) plus long-running subscription workers, documented in `docs/PRODUCTION_INFRA.md`; ARCH §4.4 is the component view. The always-on `services/keeper` second-by-second loop is retained **only** as a localnet-demo affordance (it also fabricates the Official Close via the harness mock feed, ADR-0028) and is explicitly not the deployed shape. This ADR records the trigger substrate; it does not change any lifecycle time, gate, or the strike engine.
