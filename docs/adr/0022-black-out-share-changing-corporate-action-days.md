# Black out share-changing corporate-action days

V1 creates no Outcome Market for a ticker on a Trading Day with an effective split, stock dividend, spin-off, merger, rights distribution, reorganization, or security-identity change. Two corporate-action sources are checked before issuance; ordinary cash-dividend ex-dates remain eligible and their price movement counts. A disqualifying action discovered after issuance permanently pauses new activity without mutating or replacing the Strike, preserves recovery, and—when an Official Close exists—settles the literal issued terms with a high-severity incident annotation. This avoids revisionable adjusted-history calculations while accepting both an explicit exception to daily seven-ticker coverage and the residual risk of late discovery.
