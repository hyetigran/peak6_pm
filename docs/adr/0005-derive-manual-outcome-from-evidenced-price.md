# Derive manual settlement outcomes from an evidenced price

Manual Settlement Override submits a settlement price only after the configured delay, and the program derives the winner from that price and the immutable Strike rather than accepting an arbitrary outcome. The incident record retains two independently fetched source responses, including at least one SIP-consolidated source, and they must agree exactly after normalization on ticker, Trading Day, final and unadjusted status, record identity, and Official Close; disagreement fails closed. On-chain enforcement covers the delay, exact agreement of the two submitted normalized values, winner derivation, and binding of a domain-separated evidence digest. Because the source responses are ordinary HTTP evidence rather than independently verifiable signed attestations, their authenticity remains an explicit Override Authority and runbook trust assumption. Complete evidence is retained for audit. A single isolated cold authority is acceptable on devnet; every non-demo deployment requires a multisignature Override Authority policy.
