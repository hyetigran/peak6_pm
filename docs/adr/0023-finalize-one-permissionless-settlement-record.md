# Finalize one permissionless Settlement Record per ticker and Trading Day

The canonical Settlement Record PDA is derived from ticker ID and Trading Day. The first Outcome Market creation initializes its immutable Pending header from validated schedule, prior-close, transport, quality, and delay terms; every later Strike for that tuple must match the header exactly. Finalization may then transition the result once from Pending to FinalOracle or, after its delay, FinalManual; the first valid result wins and the account is retained permanently. Anyone may pay to refresh or redeliver the same immutable public-feed record identity/revision, submit a verified normal result, and settle each Outcome Market without operator authority or private credentials. A manual result uses the same identity after its delay, with two-source agreement and canonical evidence binding; HTTP-source authenticity remains an explicit Override Authority trust assumption rather than an on-chain proof.
